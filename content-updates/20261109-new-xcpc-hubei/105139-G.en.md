# Genshin Impact Startup Forbidden II

![Genshin Impact is forbidden](hubei-105139-G-1.jpg)

Blue-edged Shot is forbidden from playing Genshin Impact by LeavingZ, so she turns to Go.

Two players take turns placing black and white stones, with black going first. The board consists of $19\times19$ intersections. An intersection $(x,y)$ is in row $x$ and column $y$. The top-left corner is $(1,1)$ and the bottom-right corner is $(19,19)$.

Intersections $(x_1,y_1)$ and $(x_2,y_2)$ are adjacent exactly when $|x_1-x_2|+|y_1-y_2|=1$. Adjacent stones of the same color belong to the same group. A stone’s number of liberties is the number of adjacent empty intersections. A group’s liberties are the sum of the liberties of all its stones. A group with zero liberties is dead and must be removed.

After black plays, first remove any dead white stones, then recalculate black’s liberties and remove dead black stones. This order matters: both colors might initially have zero liberties, but removing white stones may give black new liberties. After white plays, process the colors in the reverse order: remove dead black stones first, then recalculate white’s liberties and remove dead white stones.

The game starts with an empty board and has $m$ moves. Given each move’s position, output the number of black and white stones removed by that move. Black plays on odd-numbered moves, and white on even-numbered moves. Every move places a stone at an empty intersection. A move is allowed at any currently empty intersection, even if it would violate the rules of real Go.

## Input

The sample and the move count specify that the first line contains $m$ ($1\le m\le5\times10^5$). The following $m$ lines each contain $x_i,y_i$ ($1\le x_i,y_i\le19$), meaning that move $i$ places a stone at $(x_i,y_i)$.

Every specified intersection is empty at the time of the move.

## Output

Output $m$ lines. In the $i$-th line, output the number of black stones removed after move $i$, followed by the number of white stones removed.
