# MACARON Likes Happy Endings

MACARON is going to read a book with $n$ chapters. Chapter $i$ contains $a_i$ characters.

He wants to read the book in the next $k$ days. On each day, he either reads several consecutive chapters starting with the first unread chapter, or rests without reading. He must finish the book within $k$ days.

MACARON enjoys reading and likes happy endings, so he does not want to feel too sad. He has an unlucky number $d$, which he believes leads to a bad ending. Suppose he reads chapters $L_i$ through $R_i$ on day $i$. His sadness on that day is the number of intervals $[l,r]$ satisfying $L_i\le l\le r\le R_i$ and

$$
\bigoplus_{j=l}^{r} a_j=d
$$

The symbol $\oplus$ denotes bitwise XOR. If he rests, his sadness is $0$.

Arrange his reading schedule to minimize the total sadness over the $k$ days. Find this minimum value.

## Input

The first line contains three integers $n,k,d$ ($1\le n\le 10^5$, $1\le k\le\min(n,20)$, $0\le d\le 10^6$), denoting the number of chapters, the number of days, and the unlucky number.

The second line contains $n$ integers $a_i$ ($0\le a_i\le 10^6$), the number of characters in each chapter.

## Output

Output one integer: the minimum possible total sadness.

## Note

An optimal schedule for the example is:

- On the first day, rest; sadness is $0$.
- On the second day, read chapters $1$ through $3$; sadness is $0$.
- On the third day, read chapters $4$ through $7$; sadness is $2$.
- On the fourth day, read chapters $8$ through $10$; sadness is $1$.
