# Genshin Impact Startup Forbidden III

Blue-edged Shot is forbidden from playing Genshin Impact by LeavingZ. Today, however, LeavingZ has gone to the School of Cyber Science and Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology to win a gold medal at the 2024 International Collegiate Programming Contest in Hubei Province, China.

A single-player Genshin Impact event called “Dodoco’s Bomb-Tastic Adventure” has begun. Each game has a pond divided into an $n\times m$ grid. Cell $(i,j)$ is in row $i$, column $j$. Exactly $k$ cells contain fish. You play as Klee, the Spark Knight, who fishes using explosives.

![Dodoco’s Bomb-Tastic Adventure](hubei-105139-H-1.png)

If Klee drops a bomb at $(a,b)$, its explosion covers all cells $(x,y)$ satisfying $|x-a|+|y-b|\le1$. From every covered cell, Klee catches one fish. She may drop bombs at any location. Find the minimum number of “Jumpy Dumpty” bombs needed to catch all the fish.

## Input

The first line contains three integers $n,m,k$ ($1\le n,m\le10^3$, $1\le k\le10$), the dimensions of the grid and the number of cells containing fish.

Each of the following $k$ lines contains three integers $x_i,y_i,a_i$ ($1\le x_i\le n$, $1\le y_i\le m$, $1\le a_i\le3$), meaning that cell $(x_i,y_i)$ contains $a_i$ fish.

All input positions $(x_i,y_i)$ are distinct.

## Output

Output one integer denoting the minimum number of bombs.

## Note

One possible solution for the example drops two bombs at $(1,2)$ and another two at $(5,5)$. It can be proved that no smaller number is sufficient.
