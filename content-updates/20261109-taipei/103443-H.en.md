# A Big Project

Time limit: 1 second. Memory limit: 1024 megabytes.

## Problem Description

The ICPC company is running a big project. Its manager, Peter, divides it into $n$ subprojects and invites his colleagues to join. He finds $2n$ interested colleagues and wants all of them to participate. Each subproject will have a team of two people. Some colleagues have never worked together, and Peter hopes this project will encourage new collaborations. However, $r$ of the $n$ subprojects are essential, and he would prefer their team members to have worked together before.

You are given a list of pairs of colleagues who have collaborated before. Partition the $2n$ colleagues into $n$ pairs so that exactly $k$ pairs appear in that list and $|k-r|$ is minimized. Output $k$ and the $n$ teams.

## Input Format

For each test case, the first line contains three integers $n$, $m$, and $r$: the number of subprojects, the number of pairs in the collaboration list, and the number of essential subprojects. The colleagues are numbered from 1 through $2n$. Each of the following $m$ lines contains two integers $x$ and $y$, indicating that they have collaborated before. Consecutive integers on a line are separated by one space.

## Output Format

For each test case, output $n+1$ lines. The first line contains $k$. Each of the following $n$ lines contains the numbers $x$ and $y$ of the two members of one team. In exactly $k$ of these lines, the pair $(x,y)$ or $(y,x)$ must occur in the collaboration list. In each of the other $n-k$ lines, neither orientation may occur in the list.

## Technical Specification

- $0\le r\le n\le450$.
- $0\le m\le\binom{2n}{2}$.

## Sample 1

### Input

```text
3 5 2
1 2
6 3
4 3
2 5
5 4
```

### Output

```text
2
5 2
3 6
1 4
```
