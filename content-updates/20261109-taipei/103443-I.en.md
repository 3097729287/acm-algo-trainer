# Seesaw

Time limit: 1 second. Memory limit: 1024 megabytes.

## Problem Description

A playground has a seesaw with $S$ seats on each side, at distances $1,2,\ldots,S$ from its center. A person of weight $w$ sitting at distance $d$ produces torque of magnitude $w\times d$ on that side. The seesaw is balanced when both sides have equal total torque.

Initially every seat is occupied by a child of positive integer weight. All children on the left have odd weights, and all children on the right have even weights. Someone suggests exchanging children to balance the seesaw. However, children weighing more than $2$ refuse to move and never change their seats. Determine whether the seesaw can be balanced, and if so, the minimum number of exchanges.

For example, let $S=3$, with all left weights equal to $1$ and all right weights equal to $2$. Exchanging the children at distance $3$ on the two sides makes the left torque $1\times1+1\times2+2\times3=9$ and the right torque $2\times1+2\times2+1\times3=9$. Thus one exchange balances the seesaw.

For another example, let $S=3$. The left weights are all $1$, while the right weights at distances $1,2,3$ are $4,2,2$. First exchange the children at distance $2$ on the two sides. Then exchange the child at distance $1$ on the left with the child at distance $3$ on the right. The left torque becomes $2\times1+2\times2+1\times3=9$, and the right torque becomes $4\times1+1\times2+1\times3=9$. One exchange cannot balance this example, so two exchanges are necessary.

## Input Format

The first line contains a positive integer $T$, the number of test cases. Each test case begins with a positive integer $S$, the number of seats on each side. The next line contains $S$ odd integers giving the left-side children's weights in order of distances from $1$ through $S$. The following line contains $S$ even integers giving the right-side children's weights in the same distance order from $1$ through $S$.

## Output Format

For each test case, output the minimum number of exchanges needed to balance the seesaw, or $-1$ if it is impossible. Output one answer per line.

## Technical Specification

- $1\le T\le5$.
- $1\le S\le100$.
- Each weight $w$ is an integer satisfying $1\le w\le50000$.
