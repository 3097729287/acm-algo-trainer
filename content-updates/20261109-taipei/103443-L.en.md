# Leadfoot

Time limit: 1 second. Memory limit: 1024 megabytes.

## Problem Description

Parking can be one of the toughest challenges in obtaining a driver's license. The International Car Parking Competition (ICPC) is held every year for parking specialists from around the world. Its rules are:

- Participants are called Drivers.
- Each Driver keeps track of their own numbers of Wins and Losses.
- Every Driver starts with 0 Wins and 0 Losses.
- Two Drivers compete only when both their numbers of Wins and their numbers of Losses are equal.
- After each match, one Driver gains one Win and the other gains one Loss.
- A Driver stops competing upon reaching $n$ Wins or $m$ Losses.
- A Driver who finishes with 0 Wins and $m$ Losses receives a Leadfoot badge.

As the organizer, you must ensure that every Driver finishes the tournament. Invite a number of Drivers that allows everyone eventually to finish with either $n$ Wins or $m$ Losses. To minimize costs, invite as few Drivers as possible. Given $n$ and $m$, determine how many Leadfoot badges you must prepare.

## Input Format

The first line contains an integer $k$, the number of competitions. Each of the following lines contains two space-separated integers $n_i$ and $m_i$, the stopping conditions of one competition.

## Output Format

For each competition, output one integer: the required number of Leadfoot badges modulo 1000000007.

## Technical Specification

- $1\le k\le10^5$.
- $1\le n_i,m_i\le10^5$.

## Sample 1

### Input

```text
5
1 1
2 2
4 6
6 4
12 3
```

### Output

```text
1
1
4
16
1024
```
